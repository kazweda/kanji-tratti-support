# Kanji Tratti

**Learn kanji stroke order by tracing**

Kanji Tratti helps you learn the stroke order of Japanese kanji, one stroke at a time. The app starts with the 1,026 kanji taught in Japanese elementary schools.  
No account needed. No ads.

---

## How to practice

At the top of the menu, choose how you want to practice.

- **Watch**: see the model kanji drawn one stroke at a time, and learn the order
- **Trace**: trace each stroke with your finger. If the order, direction or shape is wrong, the app shows what was off, and you redo just that stroke

Then choose a set by school grade. Each round has 10 kanji. No kanji repeats until you have seen every kanji in the set (counted separately for Watch and Trace). Kanji you did not finish come first in the next round.

Each kanji shows its readings in kana and in romaji, its English meanings and its stroke count. The kanji are drawn in Japanese style, whatever language your device uses.

The screens are in English, or in Japanese when your device is set to Japanese.

- Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. Prices vary by region; you can check the price on the store's purchase screen
- On iPhone and iPad, Family Sharing is supported. Family members in the same group can use it at no extra cost

### How to buy

1. On the menu, tap any set from Grade 2 to Grade 6
2. Tap "Buy the Elementary Set" and confirm the purchase on the store's screen

### Restore purchases (new device or reinstall)

Your purchase is recorded in your store account. If you are signed in with the same Apple Account or Google Account you used to buy it, the sets unlock automatically when you open the app.

If they do not unlock, restore your purchase as follows.

1. On the menu, tap any set from Grade 2 to Grade 6
2. Tap "Restore purchases"

Purchases cannot be transferred between iPhone/iPad and Android, because the App Store and Google Play keep separate purchase records.

## FAQ

**Q. Can I use the app offline?**  
A. Yes. The stroke order data is built into the app. You only need an internet connection to buy or restore the purchase.

**Q. Does the app save my personal data or the lines I draw?**  
A. No. The app does not save your name or any other personal data, or the lines you draw. It only saves your purchase and your practice progress (which kanji have already appeared) on your device, and never sends them to the developer. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/en/) for details.

**Q. I bought the Elementary Set, but grades 2 to 6 are still locked.**  
A. Check that you are signed in to the store with the account you used to buy it, then tap "Restore purchases". If the sets still do not unlock, please contact us at the address below.

**Q. I would like a refund.**  
A. Purchases are processed by the store, so please request a refund from the store.
- App Store: [Request a refund from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223)
- Google Play: [Refunds on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637?hl=en)
